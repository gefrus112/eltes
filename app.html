Bring your own key

Keys are kept in this browser's local storage and sent directly to the provider you choose. They never touch an Eltes server — because there isn't one. Add any provider you use; requests go straight from this tab to the API.

Custom endpoint

Point Eltes at Ollama, vLLM, LiteLLM or your own gateway. Any OpenAI-compatible /chat/completions endpoint works — no key needed for localhost.

Requests are sent from your browser; localhost endpoints must allow CORS.
